The information gap

Rare cells can determine the outcome while disappearing inside population averages.

Endpoint assays are essential, but they usually characterize the population that remains. For resistance, persistence and therapeutic-cell function, the harder question can be what distinguished the exact cells that later survived, exhausted, recovered or remained effective.

01

Endpoints identify outcomes, not always the path

Baseline and endpoint measurements can miss transient states that occur before the fate of an individual cell becomes obvious.

02

Expansion creates material, not original-state history

Culture and expansion can produce more cells, but they cannot reconstruct the exact pretreatment state and early response of the original cell after selection has occurred.

03

Preserve the history that can change the next experiment

VitraCell studies are designed to help sponsors prioritize which cell state, treatment condition, mechanism or manufacturing variable deserves deeper investigation.

From cell state to cell fate

One cell. One identity. One continuous response record.

A VitraCell study links the state of an original viable cell before a controlled perturbation to its response over time, its independently measured fate and—where the study design supports it—its terminal molecular phenotype. Peer-reviewed scientific foundation

Rare-cell state and fate are already important across oncology and cell therapy.

The platform direction is grounded in published biology showing that rare pretreatment states can precede resistance, CAR-T persistence is central to therapeutic efficacy, tumor-reactive T-cell states evolve during TIL therapy, and viable CTCs can support downstream functional study.

01

Rare states can precede drug resistance

Lineage-tracing studies have shown that rare drug-naive cellular states can be associated with later resistant fates, supporting the value of connecting starting state with outcome.

Emert et al., Nature Biotechnology →
02

CAR-T persistence is a core biological problem

Current reviews identify persistence, exhaustion and functional state as central determinants of CAR-T performance in cancer, creating a clear research need for longitudinal therapeutic-cell characterization.

CAR T cell persistence in cancer →
03

TIL states evolve across therapy

Longitudinal single-cell and T-cell-receptor sequencing has tracked tumor-reactive T-cell plasticity through melanoma TIL therapy, reinforcing the value of state-to-fate analysis in cellular immunotherapy.

Cell Reports Medicine, 2026 →
04

Viable CTCs can support functional study

Peer-reviewed work has established the rationale for viable CTC enrichment, culture and functional investigation. Vimeran begins with this blood-based rare-cell use case.

Delta State Response

Turn same-cell history into a standardized response record.

ΔSR is VitraCell's analytical framework for connecting an individual cell's baseline, response trajectory, later fate and molecular context. In sponsor studies, that record can be used to compare response states, identify high-value follow-up populations and decide which mechanism, combination or process condition deserves deeper investigation.

Research framework · Version 1.0

A commercial research layer for oncology and cell therapy

ΔSR organizes identity-linked observations into a consistent cell-level record: baseline + perturbation + response history + fate + selected molecular identity. The framework is designed to sit around a sponsor's existing endpoint rather than replace it.
