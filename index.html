The information gap

Endpoint assays tell you what remains. State-to-fate studies preserve how individual cells got there.

Population averages, destructive endpoints and post-treatment survivor analysis are essential tools. An identity-preserved longitudinal layer adds a different view: the sequence of changes experienced by the exact cell that ultimately survives, recovers, arrests or dies.

01

Snapshots can lose the path

Baseline and endpoint measurements can show where a cell population started and where it ended without revealing the intermediate response of the exact cells that survived, recovered or died.

02

Expansion creates material, not history

Growing surviving cells can provide more material for study, but it cannot reconstruct the pretreatment state and early response of the original cell before culture and selection occurred.

03

Turn cell history into a development decision

The service is designed to help teams prioritize which condition, cell population or mechanism deserves deeper molecular, combination or translational investigation.

Peer-reviewed scientific foundation

The service is built on documented biology, not an invented need.

Independent research has established several foundations relevant to VitraCell: viable CTCs can support functional study; intact cells retain phenotypic information unavailable from cell-free analytes; treatment can induce measurable single-cell physical and electrical state changes; and pretreatment cell state can be associated with later resistance fate.

01

Viable CTCs enable functional study

Peer-reviewed work has described viable CTC enrichment, culture and functional investigation, including drug-screening applications. The scarcity of CTCs remains a major technical constraint.

Pantel & Alix-Panabières, Clinical Chemistry →
02

CTCs can complement ctDNA

Recent reviews distinguish viable CTCs from ctDNA because intact cells retain phenotypic and functional information, supporting their study as a complementary liquid-biopsy analyte.

Recent CTC precision-oncology review →
03

Drug exposure can change cell state

Single-cell impedance studies have detected heterogeneous drug-associated electrical responses, including signals observed before overt phenotypic change in experimental cancer-cell systems.

Chemical Science single-cell study →
04

Treatment changes measurable cell state

Recent mechanophenotype and single-cell electrical studies reinforce that therapeutic perturbation can produce measurable state transitions. VitraCell uses that evidence to design longitudinal studies that connect early response measurements with later fate.

2026 mechanophenotype study →

The cited studies establish the external scientific basis for the research strategy. VitraCell-sponsored studies define performance and utility for each specific model, measurement method and use case.

Computational study design

Define the failure modes before running the wet-lab experiment.

VitraCell's ΔSR computational framework formalizes how identity errors, measurement noise, sampling yield and validation strategy affect an identity-linked longitudinal analysis. That work is used to design better-controlled sponsor studies and to specify what evidence would justify advancing a measurement approach.

Computational feasibility of Delta State Response for early cell fate prediction

A synthetic methods study of identity-linked early cell-state histories, measurement noise, cell-identity errors and later simulated fate. It provides a quantitative design framework for choosing comparators, controlling identity error and validating at the experimental-run level.

The company

Founder-led, evidence-driven and built in South Carolina.

VitraCell was founded to address an information problem in precision oncology: endpoint measurements characterize what remains, while identity-preserved longitudinal studies can retain the response history that led individual cells to that outcome.

The company is building a sponsored-research business around state-to-fate study design, identity-linked analysis and, over time, Vimeran-enabled viable-cell acquisition. The commercial objective is to give translational teams a practical way to add cell history to high-value response and resistance experiments.
