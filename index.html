The VitraCell approach

The survivors have a history. We want to connect it to their fate.

Traditional endpoints tell researchers what remained. VitraCell’s ΔSR framework examines whether following the same living cell through treatment adds information worth acting on.

01 · BEFORE

Know the starting state

Record the original cell before treatment while preserving its identity.

02 · DURING

Follow its response

Connect repeated observations to that same cell during a defined perturbation.

03 · AFTER

Link to its fate

Compare its history with an independent outcome and, where feasible, molecular context.
