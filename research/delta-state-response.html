Delta State Response: turn rare-cell history into decision-ready research data.

Delta State Response (ΔSR) is VitraCell's framework for linking the baseline state of an individual living cell with how that same cell changes during a controlled perturbation, what fate it reaches and, where appropriate, its molecular phenotype. The commercial objective is to give oncology and cell-therapy teams a structured way to use cell history when deciding which cells, conditions, mechanisms or process variables deserve deeper investigation.

State-to-fate research framework · Oncology + cell therapy

From a snapshot to a response trajectory

Most single-cell assays are powerful at describing a cell at a particular moment. ΔSR adds continuity. For a cell i, the change at time t can be represented as ΔSRi(t) = Xi(t) − Xi(0), where X is a measured cell state. The commercial value is not the subtraction itself. It is the ability to preserve a verified sequence of observations from the same living cell and connect that sequence to a later outcome.

The target data object

Cell identity + baseline state + perturbation metadata + repeated response measurements + independently measured fate + selected molecular context + quality-control metadata.

Where ΔSR can create commercial value

Oncology response & resistance

Compare early response trajectories among cells that later die, persist, recover or enter a resistant state. The output can help prioritize mechanisms, combinations and survivor populations for deeper work.

Cell-therapy development

Study heterogeneity in CAR-T, TCR-T, TIL or NK-cell persistence, exhaustion, expansion and killing activity after a defined stimulus or target-cell encounter.

Scarce original material

Preserve functional history when expansion or destructive testing would remove the original state that makes a rare cell scientifically valuable.

What a sponsor can receive from a ΔSR study

  1. A study architecture tied to a real development decision: cell model, perturbation, comparator, observation window, fate endpoint and predefined success criteria.
  2. An identity-linked longitudinal dataset: repeated measurements organized at the individual-cell level rather than only as population averages.
  3. Response-state analysis: comparison of candidate trajectories or subpopulations associated with different later fates.
  4. Incremental-information analysis: determine whether the longitudinal record adds useful information beyond the sponsor's existing baseline and endpoint measurements.
  5. A decision-focused report: identify which condition, cell population, mechanism or next experiment is best supported by the resulting data.

Designed to complement the sponsor's existing assay

ΔSR is not intended to replace a trusted viability, cytotoxicity, growth, molecular or imaging endpoint. It adds a time-linked layer around that endpoint. A sponsor can keep the assay it already trusts and ask a more specific question: does knowing the path of the same cell improve the way we interpret the outcome?

Measurement-agnostic by design

ΔSR is a data and study framework rather than a requirement to use one sensor. Depending on the biological question, repeated observations may come from imaging, morphology, growth, biomass, electrical or dielectric behavior, signaling measurements or a combination of compatible nondestructive readouts. VitraCell's Vimeran development program includes dielectric characterization as one candidate modality for viable rare cells, but the sponsor's biological question should determine the measurement strategy.

Why identity preservation matters commercially

When the cell that matters is rare, its future fate can make its earlier history disproportionately valuable. A persister cell, an exhausted therapeutic lymphocyte or a rare responder may be obvious only after the experiment is over. If the original cell remained traceable, the sponsor can connect the endpoint back to what that cell was doing earlier instead of inferring the history from a different population.

Oncology example

  • Drug exposure begins.
  • Individual cells show different early responses.
  • A minority survives or recovers.
  • ΔSR links the surviving cell back to its own pretreatment and early-response record.
  • The sponsor can prioritize that response pattern for molecular or combination follow-up.

Cell-therapy example

  • Therapeutic cells encounter antigen or target cells.
  • Individual cells diverge in activation and function.
  • Some persist; others exhaust or lose activity.
  • ΔSR preserves the history leading to those different fates.
  • The sponsor can compare cell state, process condition or product design against later function.

The computational foundation

VitraCell has built a synthetic ΔSR methods model to test how identity errors, measurement noise, missing records, sampling yield and experimental-run effects influence longitudinal analysis. In the modeled informative scenario, intermediate history recovered signal that was not present in the simpler baseline-plus-endpoint comparison; in a null scenario, adding history produced little gain. That work is used to structure sponsor studies with appropriate comparators, identity controls and run-level validation.

The computational program is a study-design asset rather than biological validation. Performance and utility are established in the specific cell model, perturbation and measurement workflow used in each sponsored study.

Commercial research program
